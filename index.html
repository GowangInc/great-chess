A note on the record

History, sources & the rules we play

Tamerlane chess is a medieval large-board chess tradition associated with Timur. That association does not establish that he invented it; the familiar story that ordinary chess was “too easy” for him is not a verified fact. This is a playable reconstruction of Tamerlane chess, not the later, different “Great Chess Completed.”

Light square shading is a modern readability aid, not a claim about historical boards, which were unchequered. All piece drawings and the oval construction above are original. The ruled-oval visual language draws on illustrated Cerîde-i Adliye covers.
